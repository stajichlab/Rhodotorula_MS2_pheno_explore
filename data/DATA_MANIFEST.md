growth curve) for 275 Rhodotorula strain/isolate rows, joinable to the MS2 aligned-feature
table via `MS2_SAMPLE_Cell`/`MS2_SAMPLE_Supernatant`. Lab-generated by Christian Ona; intended
for a future metabolite-composition ~ copper-sensitivity association analysis alongside the
existing color-phenotype work. 271/275 rows are usable after excluding the 2 "No MS2 Data"
rows and the 2 conflicting C_190/SUP_190 duplicate rows (see Known Issues and
`data/metadata/rhodotorula_auc_copper/provenance.md`). Not yet used in any analysis.
